Stats Glossary

Every abbreviation used across the stats pages, in plain English.

Box-score basics (per game)

PointsPTS / PPG
Points scored per game.
ReboundsREB / RPG
Total rebounds (offensive + defensive) per game.
AssistsAST / APG
Passes that directly lead to a made basket, per game.
StealsSTL / SPG
Times a player takes the ball from the opponent, per game.
BlocksBLK / BPG
Shots deflected by a defender, per game.
TurnoversTOV
Times a player loses the ball to the opponent, per game.
MinutesMPG
Minutes played per game — a proxy for how much a team leans on a player.

Shooting

Field goal %FG%
Share of all shot attempts (except free throws) that go in.
Three-point %3P%
Share of three-point attempts that go in.
Free throw %FT%
Share of free throws made.
Threes made3PM
Made three-pointers per game.

Advanced (1996-97 onward)

True shooting %TS%
Scoring efficiency that accounts for twos, threes and free throws in one number. ~58%+ is excellent.
Usage rateUSG%
Share of a team's plays a player 'uses' (shot, free-throw trip or turnover) while on court. Stars sit around 28-35%.
Player impact estimatePIE
A player's share of the total statistical contribution in their games — a quick all-in-one value gauge. ~10% is average; 15%+ is a star.
Net ratingNetRtg
Team point differential per 100 possessions while the player is on court (positive = team outscores opponents).
Plus / minus+/−
How many points a team outscored (or was outscored by) the opponent while the player was on the floor.

This site

Rating—
Our 58–99 player rating, derived from real per-game production (points, rebounds, assists, steals, blocks and threes). Used to draft and grade teams.
Peak rating—
A player's best single-season rating across their career.
